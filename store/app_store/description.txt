Myanmar Calendar is an all-in-one daily calendar for Myanmar dates, moon phases, astrology, holidays, events and calendars you design yourself.

See the Myanmar and Western date of every day side by side, with the moon, Sabbath days, holidays and the traditional astrological days, and plan your week with events, class timetables and reminders.

KEY FEATURES
• Myanmar and Western calendar in one month grid, with the moon, holidays, Sabbath and your events on each day
• 22 calendar layouts, including Almanac, Myanmar months, Focus week, Auspice and Moon band, and 12 grid themes
• Day details: Myanmar date, Sasana year, moon phase and countdown to the next full moon, new moon and Sabbath
• Astrology in plain words: Sabbath, Yatyaza, Pyatthada, Mahabote, Nakhat, year name and the Nagahle compass
• Holidays: search and filter every public, religious and cultural holiday, and get reminders
• Year, month, week, day, Myanmar month and agenda views
• Date converter, date difference and date arithmetic tools
• Events with reminders, repeats and categories
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets in five styles
• Myanmar and English interface; calendar data in Myanmar, English, Zawgyi, Mon, Shan and Karen

CLASS TIMETABLES AND MULTIPLE DATES
Set up a weekly timetable in one go: pick the weekdays (say, every Wednesday and Thursday), give each day its own time, choose when the term ends, and add a reminder. For irregular days such as exams or make-up classes, pick any set of dates on the Myanmar calendar and save them as one event.

CALENDAR STUDIO
Design your own wall, desk, social or story calendar from ready-made presets. Edit text, images, shapes, decorations, calendar grids and holiday legends right on the page, restyle every page with a project theme, and export to PDF, PNG, JPEG or ZIP at each page's real paper size.

BUILT FOR EVERYDAY USE
• Fast, local-first and works offline
• Clean, modern design with light and dark themes, custom colours and ten app icons
• Clear setup on first launch, and a "What's new" note after each update
• Privacy controls with first-run consent for analytics and crash reporting
