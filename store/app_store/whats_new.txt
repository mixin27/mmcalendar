Version 2.7 brings Myanmar Calendar to your wrist.

APPLE WATCH
Today's Myanmar date, the moon, holidays and Sabbath days, swipe through the days ahead, and see what's coming up. Add a complication to your watch face for the date or the moon. Your iPhone keeps the watch up to date, and it carries on for months on its own.

LONG WEEKENDS
Holidays › Long weekends lists this year's breaks and the days of leave that make them longer: take one day off, get five. Share them with family and friends.

MERIT DIARY
Log donations, precepts, meditation and other merit on any day. Views › Merit diary shows your Sabbath streak, this year's totals and every entry by month.

SHARE AN EVENT
Share any event as a QR code, a link or an .ics file. Whoever opens it sees the event, Myanmar-date repeats included, and can add it to their calendar.

WIDGETS YOU CAN TAP
Tick an event done straight from the Agenda and Today widgets, and open the note editor from the Today's note widget.
