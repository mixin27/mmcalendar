## 📖 Overview

**flutter_mmcalendar** is a set of Myanmar calendar widgets for Flutter, built on [`myanmar_calendar_dart`](https://pub.dev/packages/myanmar_calendar_dart) 3. Every widget shows both the Western and the Myanmar calendar, plus what the core computes for the day: holidays by type, Sabbath, moon phases, astrology, the Shan and Sasana years and the chronicles.

Version **4.0** is a rewrite of every widget. The calendar UI now renders as **Material 3**, **Cupertino** or **Liquid Glass** from one scope and one theme. A single `MyanmarCalendarScope` near the top of the app gives every calendar widget below it a shared client (configuration, custom holidays and the day cache), language, style and theme. The latest release, **4.0.1**, makes room for Myanmar script in stacked labels so vowel and tone marks no longer overlap.

The package re-exports `myanmar_calendar_dart`, so one import gives both the widgets and the calendar API. It runs on Android, iOS, macOS, Windows, Linux and the web, and needs Flutter 3.41 or later.

## 🎨 Adaptive styles

One `MmVisualStyle` decides how everything looks. Set it once on the scope, or pass `style:` to a single widget.

| Style | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| **Material 3** | Tonal surfaces from the app's `ColorScheme`, Material buttons, segmented buttons and dialogs. |
| **Cupertino** | Grouped lists, hairline edges, `CupertinoButton`, sliding segmented controls and sheets with a grabber. |
| **Liquid Glass** | Translucent panels that bend what is behind them at the rim, capsule controls and floating sheets. |

`MmVisualStyle.automatic` picks Glass on iOS and macOS 26 and later, Cupertino on earlier iOS and macOS, and Material everywhere else.

Liquid Glass is drawn in pure Flutter, with no platform views or native code:

- **On Impeller**, `MmGlass` runs the backdrop through a refraction fragment shader bundled with the package (`shaders/liquid_glass.frag`). `MmGlass.precache()` can load it before the first frame.
- **On Skia, on the web, and while a route is animating**, it falls back to a blur with a tint and a bright edge.
- **With high contrast turned on**, it draws an opaque surface instead.

The adaptive building blocks the widgets are made of are public too: `MmSurface`, `MmGlass`, `MmButton`, `MmIconButton`, `MmChip`, `MmSegmented`, `MmListGroup` / `MmListRow`, `showMmSheet` and `showMmPopover`, so the rest of a screen can match the calendar.

## ✨ Widget catalogue

### 📅 Calendars

- **`MyanmarMonthCalendar`**: a month that pages by swiping, with a header showing the Western month and the Myanmar months it spans. Five cell styles (`dual`, `westernFirst`, `lunarFirst`, `minimal`, `moon`), holidays coloured by type, Sabbath, events and full and new moons marked. Single, range and multi selection through `MyanmarCalendarController`, plus arrow keys, Page Up/Down and Home/End.
- **`MyanmarWeekStrip`**: one week in a row, paging by swiping.
- **`MyanmarMiniMonth`**: a compact month for sidebars and the year view.
- **`MyanmarYearCalendar`**: twelve mini months with holidays and full moons.
- **`MyanmarLunarMonthCalendar`**: a Myanmar month laid out as it is counted, waxing 1–15 then waning, with First and Second Waso in watat years.
- **`MyanmarAgenda` / `MyanmarAgendaSliver`**: the days with holidays, anniversaries, Sabbath, moon days or your events, with a pinned header per month.

### 🗓️ Pickers

- **`showMyanmarDatePicker`**, **`showMyanmarDateRangePicker`**, **`showMyanmarMultiDatePicker`**: Material shows a dialog; Cupertino and Glass show a sheet with Cancel and Done. Each has a Western / Myanmar switch and a month-year grid behind the calendar title.
- **`MyanmarDatePicker`**: the picker's content, for use inline.
- **`MyanmarDateWheelPicker`**: year, month and fortnight-day wheels that only offer days that exist.
- **`MyanmarMonthYearPicker`**: a month grid for jumping to a month.
- **`MyanmarDateFormField`**: a `FormField<CompleteDate>` with validation.

### 📋 Day information

- **`MyanmarDateText`**: a date formatted with the core's patterns; `.today()` turns over at midnight.
- **`MyanmarTodayCard`**: the day at a glance; `.compact` is a home-screen-sized square.
- **`MyanmarDayDetails`**: dates, moon, holidays and astrology in sections, each date with a copy button.
- **`HolidayBadge`, `HolidayList.upcoming`**: holidays coloured by type, and a countdown to the next ones.
- **`AstroBadges`, `AstrologyCard`, `NagahleCompass`**: Sabbath, Yatyaza, Pyathada, mahabote, nakhat, the year name and the dragon's direction.

### 🌕 Moon

- **`MoonGlyph`** (with `MoonPainter`): a drawn moon for any day.
- **`MoonPhaseStrip`**: a month's quarter days.
- **`NextMoonCountdown`**: days until the next full moon, new moon and Sabbath.

### 🧰 Tools

- **`MyanmarDateConverter`**: converts between Western and Myanmar dates, with a swap button.
- **`DateDifferenceCard`**: the days between two dates, and a date plus days or Myanmar months.
- **`AIPromptAction`**: builds horoscope, fortune or divination prompts and hands them to your callback; it never copies or sends anything itself.
- **`ChronicleCard`**: the eras running on a date and the dated records near it.

## 🚀 Quick start

Add the package:

```yaml
dependencies:
  flutter_mmcalendar: ^4.0.0
```

Wrap the app in a `MyanmarCalendarScope`, then drop in any widget:

```dart
import 'package:flutter/material.dart';
import 'package:flutter_mmcalendar/flutter_mmcalendar.dart';

void main() {
  // Optional: load the glass shader before the first frame.
  MmGlass.precache();
  runApp(
    MyanmarCalendarScope(
      language: Language.myanmar, // leave out to follow the app's locale
      style: MmVisualStyle.automatic, // or material, cupertino, glass
      child: MaterialApp(
        theme: ThemeData(colorSchemeSeed: Colors.indigo),
        home: const HomePage(),
      ),
    ),
  );
}
```

A month calendar with range selection, a Myanmar-first cell style and events:

```dart
MyanmarMonthCalendar(
  controller: MyanmarCalendarController(selection: RangeSelection()),
  cellStyle: MyanmarDayCellStyle.lunarFirst,
  firstDayOfWeek: DateTime.monday,
  eventLoader: (day) => events[day] ?? const [],
  onSelectionChanged: (selection) { /* ... */ },
  dayBuilder: (context, day, cell) => cell, // wrap or replace a cell
)
```

Pick a date with the Myanmar wheels:

```dart
final CompleteDate? day = await showMyanmarDatePicker(
  context: context,
  initialDate: DateTime.now(),
  inputMode: MyanmarPickerInput.wheels, // or calendar
);
```

## 🧩 Design notes

- **Theming follows the app.** Colours come from `MmCalendarThemeData`, a `ThemeExtension`. Without one, widgets build it from the app's `ColorScheme`, so light and dark mode follow the app. Holiday, Sabbath and moon colours are fixed per brightness so they mean the same thing in every app. A custom theme is one line: `MmCalendarThemeData.fromSeed(Colors.teal).copyWith(cellRadius: 8)` in `ThemeData.extensions`.
- **Six languages.** English and Myanmar are complete; Shan, Mon, Karen and Zawgyi have the core's date names, with widget labels addable through `TranslationService.addTranslation`. Digits follow the language, and screen readers hear each day in the calendar's language.
- **Myanmar typography.** The theme sets a Myanmar font fallback (Noto Sans Myanmar, Myanmar Sangam MN, Padauk) and the taller line height Myanmar script needs. Since 4.0.1, `MmCalendarThemeData.lineHeight` gives the line height a stacked label needs in each language, so the agenda, week strip and holiday list keep vowel and tone marks clear.
- **Correct days everywhere.** Days are looked up by their civil date, so the device's time zone can't shift a cell to the neighbouring day.
- **Accessible by default.** Screen-reader labels in the calendar's language, text that scales without overflowing, and right-to-left support.
- **A clean migration path.** 4.0 replaces every 3.x widget; the README has a one-to-one migration table (for example `MyanmarCalendarWidget` becomes `MyanmarMonthCalendar` with a controller).
