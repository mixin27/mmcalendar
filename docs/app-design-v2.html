Myanmar Calendar · design v2

A proposal to move the whole app off stock Material onto its own identity: Lacquer & paper. It uses warm paper surfaces, ink text and hairlines instead of shadows. Jade is the action colour, lacquer red marks holidays, and gold marks the moon and Sabbath. Myanmar serif type sets headings. Every frame uses real data for October 2026 (သီတင်းကျွတ်, ME 1388). The home grid stays as it is for now, and two replacements are proposed below.

Calendar grids

The same month in three designs. All three use real lunar data, and the selected day is 22 October. Calm reads like a wall calendar and puts lunar details on demand. Rich keeps the lunar day in every cell, the way people use the current grid, but in a quieter, ruled layout. Both fix the moon colours that break in dark mode today.

ACurrent

Bordered tiles, four coloured dots, a light amber or indigo moon fill, and a purple Material "today" marker. It is dense, and every cell competes for attention equally.

BCalm (proposed)

No cell borders. Today is a solid ink disc, and holidays and Sundays use red numerals. Marks under each date: gold dot for Sabbath, gold disc for full moon, ring for new moon, blue for the user's events. Tapping a day shows its Myanmar date in a panel.

Build plan

This is the order for the implementation round, once a grid is chosen.

StepWhat changesWhere
1 · TokensAppTokens ThemeExtension (light and dark), with one AppTheme.build(tokens) replacing the duplicated themes. Component themes are derived from the tokens. Custom colour presets map onto the jade role.shared_ui_kit/theme
2 · ComponentsAppTabBar, AppRail, AppHeader (sliver and fixed), AppCard, AppListRow, AppButton (primary, secondary, ghost, danger), AppChip, AppSegmented, AppSheet, AppField, AppEmptyState. AppToast already exists.shared_ui_kit/widgets
3 · ShellThe floating tab bar and rail are built from one destination list, the "TG: user" bar moves into Settings, and the breakpoints are 600 and 1024.app_shell.dart
4 · HomeThe Today card, one month switcher (no date-jump button), the Upcoming list and the Studio tile. The grid gets token colours only, until a new grid is chosen.features/calendar
5 · TabsViews, Converter, Events and Settings move to AppHeader and AppListRow. Hard-coded colours become tokens.feature packages
6 · New gridCalm or Rich, behind a display setting so people can switch back for a release.calendar_grid.dart, date_cell.dart
