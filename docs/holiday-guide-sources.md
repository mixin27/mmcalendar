# Holiday guide: sources and notes

These are the sources for the "Good to know" text on holiday pages, in `packages/features/holidays/lib/src/guide/holiday_guide_{en,my}.dart`. The text is written in our own words; facts come from the pages below. Wikipedia and Wikivoyage are CC BY-SA, so we take facts from them, never wording.

**Rules for the text:**
- No prices, opening hours or events tied to a single year.
- Write "usually" or "often" where practice varies.
- National days are described neutrally: what the day marks and what closes.
- Anything only one weak source supports, or that sources disagree on, is left out. Leaving a gap is better than guessing.

## Shared

- Pagoda etiquette (shoes and socks off across the whole compound, shoulders and knees covered, longyi rental at the gate, walking clockwise, not pointing your feet at Buddha images or monks, giving monks food rather than money, women not touching monks):
  - https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Myanmar
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etiquette_in_Myanmar
- Public holidays and how long they last: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_holidays_in_Myanmar

## Thingyan

- **Sources:**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thingyan
  - https://my.wikipedia.org/wiki/သင်္ကြန်
  - https://www.bestpricetravel.com/travel-guide/thingyan-festival-2461.html
  - https://www.nomadasaurus.com/?p=823
- **Covered:** 4–5 days ending on New Year's Day; washing away bad luck; pandals, yein, thangyat, free food (satuditha); keeping precepts, washing elders' hair, releasing fish; pandals at Kandawgyi Lake and Kabaraye Road (Yangon) and around the palace moat with floats (Mandalay).
- **Left out:**
  - Exact transport and shop opening patterns (single source; one guide says malls stay open).
  - The number of pandals in Mandalay in a given year.
  - Recent security events.
- **Disputed:** guides differ on whether elders and babies are spared. We say "most people also spare".

## Thadingyut

- **Sources:**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thadingyut_Festival
  - Wikivoyage (above)
- **Covered:** ends Lent; three nights of lights recalling the descent from Tavatimsa; paying respects to elders; theatre, fairs and food; floating lamps at Shwegyin and floating alms bowls at Dawei.
- **Left out:**
  - How many days off there are (it varies by year; Wikipedia lists 9 for one year).
  - Kyaukse elephant dance timing (sources disagree).
  - Power cuts (unverified).
  - Which Mandalay pagoda is busiest (sources disagree).

## Tazaungdaing

- **Sources:**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tazaungdaing_festival
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taunggyi_Hot_Air_Balloon_Festival
  - https://tourism.gov.mm/en/festivals/hot-air-balloon-festival
  - https://mekongtourism.org/events/kahtein-robe-weaving-competitions-myanmar/
  - https://time.com/4133001/hot-air-balloon-fireworks-burma/
  - RFA Burmese photo reports of the matho thingan contests at Shwedagon (2024) and Botahtaung (2023)
- **Covered:** end of the kathina season; lanterns and fire balloons; overnight matho thingan robe weaving offered before dawn; mezali-bud salad; pranks on the full-moon night; a two-day holiday; the Taunggyi festival lasts about a week, draws tens of thousands, and balloons have fallen into crowds; seinnapan lantern balloons and balloons carrying fireworks at night.
- **Left out:**
  - The ground's name.
  - Which balloon shapes fly by day and which by night (Wikipedia and the tourism ministry give them the opposite way round).
  - Whether the robe goes to a Buddha image or to monks (sources disagree).
  - Hotel availability (no source).

## Tabaung (Full moon of Tabaung)

- **Sources:**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabaung
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sand_pagoda
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shwedagon_Pagoda
  - https://tourism.gov.mm/en/festivals/shwedagon-pagoda-festival
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peasants%27_Day
- **Covered:** pagoda-festival season; the Shwedagon festival; five-tier sand pagodas in Upper Myanmar; Peasants' Day (2 March) is a separate holiday that sometimes falls on the same day; Shwesettaw and Kakku.
- **Left out:**
  - How long the Shwedagon festival lasts (sources disagree).
  - Robe weaving at Tabaung (that is a Tazaungmon custom).
  - Pa-O National Day (single source).

## Shan New Year

- **Sources:**

## Myanmar text: please review

- **ရေကစားမဏ္ဍပ်** (pandal): no source page uses this exact term; it's the common word.
- **ဖားစည်** (frog drum): no Myanmar-language source found.
- **ရှမ်းဗုံရှည်** (Shan long drum): check that this is the usual name.
- **ကျီးမနိုး** (the Tazaungdaing pranks): check that the phrasing reads naturally.
- **စိန်နားပန်း** (lantern balloons): this follows the English "seinnapan"; check the spelling.
