# Holiday guide: sources and notes

These are the sources for the "Good to know" text on holiday pages, in `packages/features/holidays/lib/src/guide/holiday_guide_{en,my}.dart`. The text is written in our own words; facts come from the pages below. Wikipedia and Wikivoyage are CC BY-SA, so we take facts from them, never wording.

**Rules for the text:**
- No prices, opening hours or events tied to a single year.
- Write "usually" or "often" where practice varies.
- National days are described neutrally: what the day marks and what closes.
- Anything only one weak source supports, or that sources disagree on, is left out. Leaving a gap is better than guessing.

## Thingyan

- **Sources:**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thingyan
  - https://my.wikipedia.org/wiki/သင်္ကြန်
  - https://www.bestpricetravel.com/travel-guide/thingyan-festival-2461.html
  - https://www.nomadasaurus.com/?p=823
- **Covered:** 4–5 days ending on New Year's Day; washing away bad luck; pandals, yein, thangyat, free food (satuditha); keeping precepts, washing elders' hair, releasing fish; pandals at Kandawgyi Lake and Kabaraye Road (Yangon) and around the palace moat with floats (Mandalay).
- **Left out:**
  - Exact transport and shop opening patterns (single source; one guide says malls stay open).
  - The number of pandals in Mandalay in a given year.
  - Recent security events.
- **Disputed:** guides differ on whether elders and babies are spared. We say "most people also spare".

## Tazaungdaing

## Corrected explanations (`holidayAbout*`)

- **New Year's Day:** it said "a public holiday since 2018". Sources show 2018–2021, then again from 2025. `myanmar_calendar_dart` 3.0.2 still marks 2022–2024 as holidays, so it needs a follow-up fix.
- **New Year's Eve:** it said "a public holiday since 2018". It was a holiday in 2017–2021, and since then only an occasional bridge day.
- **Garudhamma:** it said the day recalls the Buddha giving the eight rules to the first nuns. No source ties the Myanmar observance to nuns, so it now describes the day as a Lent full moon kept with precepts and offerings.
- **Mon National Day:** it said "falls on the full moon of Tabodwe". It falls the day after. The calendar package already gives the right date.
