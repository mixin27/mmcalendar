Myanmar Calendar · design v4

Three new features, drawn in the v3 system: a first-run onboarding that ends with a short setup, a What's new sheet and history that replace the promo carousel, and a Holidays page to browse, search and be reminded of holidays. Every screen uses the real calendar data for 2026, with today as Sunday 18 October (သီတင်းကျွတ် လဆန်း ၇, ME 1388). Use the buttons to see the Material (Android) style and dark mode.

1 · Onboarding

Shown once, on first launch. Four short tour pages say what the app does, then four setup steps make the app the user's own. Every setup step works with "Not now", so someone in a hurry reaches the calendar in eight taps. The consent page and the first-frame notification prompt go away: both become setup steps. People who already use the app never see this.

Update check→Tour · 4 pages→Setup · 4 steps→All set→Today

2 · What's new

After an update, a sheet shows that version's new features, one page each, with a button that opens the feature. If someone skipped versions, the sheet combines them, newest first. New installs never see it, since onboarding already covered the app. The full history stays in Settings › About › What's new. The content ships with the app and is translated like every other screen.

App updated→Onboarding done?→Sheet: new items since last seen→"Try it" opens the feature

3 · Holidays

A Holidays page opened from the Views tab and from any holiday row in the app. Holidays that span days show as one entry (Thingyan runs 13–21 April, Thadingyut 25–27 October), each with a short explanation of what it marks. Search works in Myanmar and English across the year, filters narrow to public, religious, cultural or anniversary days, and any holiday can remind you the evening before.
