Calendar Grid Themes

Twelve themes for the home calendar, in the spirit of the Calendar Studio presets. A theme is a second choice next to the grid layout: it brings its own paper, ink, accent, number typeface and a small motif in the header corner, and works with every one of the 15 layouts. Each theme has a light and a dark version. The day sheet and tab bar keep the app’s colours. “App colours” stays the default, so nothing changes until you pick a theme. Mark which ones to build and whether motifs should be on by default, then copy your picks back to me.
