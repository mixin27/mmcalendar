0 · As built

Built and running: nine home-screen widgets on both platforms, four iPhone lock-screen widgets, and five styles. Each widget's style is set per widget. Captures are real, on the same data as below: iPhone 17 Pro simulator (iOS 17 WidgetKit) and an Android 15 emulator (Jetpack Glance).

  • Taps open the day, month, event or new-event screen. The Month arrows move the month inside the widget.
  • Edit Widget (iPhone) or the widget's settings (Android) sets its style and, for Countdown, its target. Settings › Widgets sets the default style and what widgets show, and on Android adds any widget to the home screen.
  • The date rolls over at midnight without opening the app. iPhone uses daily timeline entries; Android schedules a refresh for each of the next seven midnights.
  • Not built: Android lock-screen widgets (no public API) and Pixel's At a Glance (closed to apps; the 4×1 Today line replaces it). Per-widget language and per-widget show toggles are app-wide settings for now.

2 · Proposed home-screen widgets

Every widget opens the app at the right place when tapped (the day, the month, the event). Sizes are real points: iPhone small 170, medium 364×170, large 364×382; Android cells resize freely and the layout adapts.

3 · Lock screen, StandBy and At a Glance

iOS renders lock-screen widgets in one tint over the wallpaper. StandBy shows a large widget on a charging phone at night, in red. Android 14+ offers lock-screen widgets on some devices; Pixel’s At a Glance can show a short line.

4 · Styles

Each widget can follow the app, use one of the calendar’s grid themes, or stay plain. Use the Style switch above to see them on every widget.

5 · Per-widget settings

Long-press a widget, Edit Widget on iPhone or Reconfigure on Android, to change it without opening the app.
