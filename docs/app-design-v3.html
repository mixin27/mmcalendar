Myanmar Calendar · design v3.1

v3.1 adds: two app styles, Clear (iOS-like) and Material (Android), switchable with the button below and in Settings › Appearance; a home page where the month always fits on a phone; a Calendar grid preference with Detailed as the default; and one real-sky moon drawing everywhere.

This is the second proposal, and it shows every screen in the app. The style is clean, modern and iOS-like: grouped white surfaces on light grey (true black in dark mode), large bold titles, and one blue accent for actions. Red is used only for holidays, amber only for the moon and Sabbath. Myanmar text is set in Padauk, paired with Inter for Latin text and numbers. There are no serif headings and no cream backgrounds. Every screen uses real data for October 2026 (သီတင်းကျွတ်, ME 1388), and every feature in today's app has a place.

App-wide

First launch, announcements, required updates, errors and the countdown toast.

Home

On a phone the month always fits: a compact header, the grid sized to the space that is left, and a day sheet peeking above the tab bar. The large Today card and the date-jump "search" button are gone; tapping the month title opens the picker. Day details keep every section the app has today.

Calendar grids

Users choose the grid in Settings › Display › Calendar grid. Detailed is the default; Clean and Classic (today’s grid, recoloured from tokens) are the alternatives. All three take their row height from the layout, so they fit any screen. Today is 16 October and 22 October is selected.

Moon

One moon drawing everywhere (grid, day details, week view, converter, widgets and Studio), with no emoji or icon stand-ins. The shape follows the real sky at 18:30 Myanmar time: the lit fraction comes from the moon’s phase angle, and the terminator is an ellipse, so quarters are true half discs and gibbous moons bulge correctly. It is tilted as the moon lies near 20°N: lit toward the lower right while waxing, toward the upper left while waning. Labels such as လပြည့် and ဥပုသ် still follow the Myanmar calendar.

Views

Year, week and day views. Day view becomes the same screen as Day Details, so it gains events, sharing and the AI prompt instead of duplicating them.

Converter

The four tools become a segmented control at the top. Every result gets Copy and Share, which today's app doesn't have.

Events

The list, search, quick actions, the full form, detail and categories. Categories finally get an entry point (Events ⋯ menu), plus edit and delete.

Settings

Every settings page and dialog, as grouped lists. Choices that used to open radio dialogs become inline checkmark lists or segmented controls.

Home-screen widgets

The four existing widgets restyled to match: Compact Date, Moon Phase, Full Calendar and Myanmar Month. The widget themes from Widget Settings still apply.

Calendar Studio

Studio keeps its print-focused canvas. Its chrome (title bars, sheets and lists) adopts this system so it feels part of the app.

Desktop and tablet

At 600 px and wider a sidebar replaces the tab bar. At 1024 px and wider the selected day opens in a detail panel; the app already does this at 1000 px.
